Why

Every match should mean something

Almost every matchmade game has the same problem: you play, the scoreboard closes, and it's gone. We want the community to live right in the lobby: log in, see people you know, see how many are playing, feel that things are happening in the background.

The inspiration is Helldivers 2, Frontline and other games with a global story, where what players do moves the story forward.

A sector is an operation

Not one map but a chain: a breakthrough on 5CP, an advance on Payload, an assault on A/D. Clear the last stage and the sector is yours.

It widens with the player count

Few players, one front. More players, several. Even eight people should feel like they're fighting a war, not sitting on empty servers.

Pick a side

At the start of a campaign you join RED or BLU and stay with them until it ends. A new campaign starts a day after the last one.

Conditions and orders

Battles can be fought with no crits, low gravity, grappling hooks or odd respawn waves. A battle on the war's order pays bonus XP, win or lose.

What's new

Everything around the shooting

Our own coordinator

Valve's Game Coordinator isn't in the SDK, so we wrote our own. Parties are never split, teams are balanced by size and rating, and a match only forms when a server is free for it.

Regions with real ping

RU/Asia, EU/Germany, NA/Canada. Your game pings our servers itself, and a match goes only where every player gets a decent ping. No more Russia to Canada.

Back-to-back matches

After a map the lobby rolls on to the next one. No new search, no lost teammates.

Clans that matter

Play and your clan levels up, faster with clanmates. Perks: bonus XP, your banner on the war map, a weekly contract, the clan's own order.

A lobby that's alive

Global, clan and party chat in the main menu. See who's online and what they're doing. A "looking for a squad" button.

Quests and career

Three daily quests and a weekly one, from kills, damage, healing, wins and classes. XP, levels and match results.

Fair teams, no ranked

There's no ranked mode. Elo is there only to make teams even: everyone starts at 1500 and is measured against the other team.

A brand-new UI

Every menu and HUD rebuilt from scratch on RmlUi: home, play, war, clan, career, quests, locker, results. Two HUDs to choose from.

Easy Anti-Cheat

EAC replaces VAC. It's still being tested, but TF2 has a cheater problem, and solving it is worth the try.

An evening in Frontress

You log in. Twenty people are already in chat, and someone from your clan invites you to a party. On the war map BLU is pushing on the Foundry, and tonight there's a battle there on orders. You hit Play, and a minute later you're on Payload, one stage of the offensive. You win: the offensive moves, a line shows up in the feed, your clan gets XP and a quest is done. The next map rolls in on its own.

Every match means something. That's the whole idea.

Built on

Thanks, Team Comtress 2

Team Frontress is a fork of Team Comtress 2, which fixes TF2's bugs, makes it faster and adds quality of life. From them we got:

  • Hundreds of bug fixes
  • Faster menus and threaded rendering fixes
  • Subtick aim: your shot uses your aim between ticks
  • A reworked crosshair, glow outlines, FOV fixes
  • Dozens of MvM fixes

Being upfront

What's not there yet

  • Ranked. Only casual and the war.
  • A big player base. It's a small project, built to be fun with ten people.
  • A finished anti-cheat. EAC is wired in but not proven in production yet.
  • Much gameplay of our own beyond TC2. It's coming.
